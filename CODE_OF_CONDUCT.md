# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AIR Club UdeSA is committed to providing an open, respectful, and safe community where people can learn and build together, regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or prior education.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and empathy.
- Welcome questions and mistakes as part of learning.
- Give specific feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit contributions, sources, and authorship.
- Protect privacy and information that is not yet public.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, publishing private information, unsolicited sexual content, or any behavior that makes participation unsafe for others will not be tolerated.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repositories, events, channels, and spaces where a person represents AIR Club UdeSA. Organization administrators may moderate content, end interactions, or restrict participation when necessary.

Report an incident privately to [airclub@udesa.edu.ar](mailto:airclub@udesa.edu.ar). Reports may be written in Spanish or English. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.
