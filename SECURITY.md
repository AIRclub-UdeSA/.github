# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a vulnerability, exposed credential, or issue that could affect people, robots, or infrastructure, do not open a public issue.

Email [airclub@udesa.edu.ar](mailto:airclub@udesa.edu.ar) with the subject `SECURITY`. Reports may be written in Spanish or English and should include:

- the affected repository and version or commit;
- a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
- minimal reproduction steps;
- any mitigation you have already tested.

Do not access other people's data, disrupt services, or extend testing beyond what is necessary to describe the issue.

The team will acknowledge the report when possible and coordinate publication of a fix before details are disclosed.
